# Changelog

All notable changes to **Peterwolf's Groundworks Dump Truck** are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

---

## [0.1.1+26.3] - 2026-10-08

### Added
- Load-based exhaust smoke plumes and heavy excavator-style diesel acoustics.
- Complete cab interior modeling with driver seat alignment and visible steering controls.
- Full AutoTrench fleet coordination with Peterwolf's Groundworks Excavator (auto-advancing one block upon excavator horn signal).
- Volumetric bed dumping with tilt angle threshold and gradual material discharge.

---

## [0.1.0+26.3] - 2026-10-06

### Added
- Initial release of **Peterwolf's Groundworks Dump Truck** for Minecraft 26.3 Fabric.
- Heavy off-road construction dump truck with functional hydraulic tipping bed.
- Granular soil loading and transportation system integrated with Groundworks Core.
